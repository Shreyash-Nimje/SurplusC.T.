02 — The Problem

67M
TONNES
WASTED

Every year, India throws away food worth ₹92,000 crore. We don't have a food shortage. We have a food sharing problem.

PROBLEM 01

🌾 Farmers throw away crops

When prices crash, farmers are forced to leave crops on the field to rot because transport costs more than the crop's worth.

PROBLEM 02

🏙️ Urban waste

Weddings and hotels prepare more food than eaten. Every day, good food goes into the bin while hungry families live streets away.

The Farmer
